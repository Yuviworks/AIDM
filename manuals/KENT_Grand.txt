Recommended Uses of Rejected Water

Rejected water has high concentration of salts and usually goes down the drain, but if required, can be
used for gardening purposes. It has high concentration of salts and minerals which accelerate plant growth
Rejected water can also be used for cleaning purposes, i.e. utensils cleaning, mopping the floor etc
